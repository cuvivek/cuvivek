### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Senior Performance Engineer (Senior Engineer II at American Express)** with over a decade of experience architecting end-to-end Non-Functional Testing (NFT) strategies, distributed system scalability, and enterprise resiliency across the **Banking, Telecom, and Energy** domains.

- 🏆 **American Express Centurion Award 2025 Winner:** Highest annual engineering recognition at Amex for technical excellence, resilience engineering, and payment authorization scalability.
- ⚡ **High-Throughput Distributed Architecture:** Scaled American Express Payment Network Authorization systems to **6,000+ Transactions Per Second (TPS)** via deep microservices tuning, thread/heap dump profiling, and workload modeling.
- 🤖 **Autonomous AI Performance Agents:** Architected and deployed an internal AI Performance Agent using **LangGraph**, Vector Databases, and Ollama/Open-Source LLMs to automate test execution, telemetry analysis, and conversational RAG diagnostics.
- 🛡️ **Chaos & Resiliency Engineering:** Dockerized **Toxiproxy** to simulate network latency, jitter, and packet loss, uncovering resilience gaps across distributed services.
- 🔄 **Shift-Left Continuous Performance:** Embedded automated performance and latency quality gates in **GitHub Actions** CI/CD pipelines.
